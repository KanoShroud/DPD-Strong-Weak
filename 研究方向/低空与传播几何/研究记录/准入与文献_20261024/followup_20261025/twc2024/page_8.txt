  The second benchmark we are considering is a maximum

in [22]. In this method, the authors came up with a simple
method that utilizes maximum-likelihood estimation over the
grid  to  directly estimate the  location of the source  in a

received signal at each access point given the known values
of the AoA and ToA on a grid point. This algorithm needs
statistical channel knowledge.
