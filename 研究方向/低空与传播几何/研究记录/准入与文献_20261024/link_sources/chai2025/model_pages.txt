                           The essential steps of the proposed algorithm are summarized below:
                                   1.    Select the reference signal x1(n) based on the prior information and segment the
                                   signals received by each sensor.
                                   2.   According to Equation (4), compute the cross-correlation between any two sensors
                           and obtain the cross-spectrum by applying the DFT.
                                   3.    Select the effective portion of the cross-spectrum according to Equation (17), and then
                              perform forward spatial smoothing to obtain ˜Rij according to Equation (18).
                                   4.   Obtain the initial position estimation pini based on Equations (22)–(24).
                                   5.   Obtain the final position estimation ˆp based on Equations (25) and (29)–(33).
