5. Numerical simulations

   In this section, through the case simulations of multiple-sinusoids acoustic sources and Gaussian pulse acoustic sources and Monte
Carlo simulations, we analyze the acoustic source identification performance of the BOMP-based multi-frequency synchronous on-grid
compressive beamforming (denoted as BOMP-CB) and the BNOMP-based multi-frequency synchronous off-grid compressive beam­
forming (denoted as BNOMP-CB), and compares them with the orthogonal matching pursuit (OMP)-based and NOMP-based single-
frequency on- and off-grid compressive beamforming (denoted as OMP-CB and NOMP-CB). Among them, for the case simulation of
multiple-sinusoids sound sources, we also present the source identification results of the state-of-the-art methods based on multiple-
snapshot measurement (such as function beamforming (FB) [50], DAMAS [8] and CLEAN-SC [13]) as a comparison. For the transient
Gaussian pulse acoustic sources based on single-snapshot measurement (which is equivalent to the case of coherent sources), FB,
DAMAS and CLEAN-SC show poor performance [2, 7, 50] and are not involved in the comparison.
