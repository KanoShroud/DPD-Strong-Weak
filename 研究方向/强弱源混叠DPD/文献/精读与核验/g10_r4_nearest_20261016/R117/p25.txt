7. Conclusions

   In this paper, a multi-frequency synchronous off-grid compressive beamforming is proposed. We first establish a maximum like­
lihood estimation model with the source coordinates and the source strengths of all frequencies as parameters, and then develop a
BNOMP solution algorithm for this model. The proposed method can overcome the basis mismatch issue. It is compatible with
arbitrary geometry planar array and is applicable to both stationary and nonstationary sources. Besides, benefiting from the sup­
pression of mutual interference between sources by global cyclic feedback optimization and the sparsity promotion by multi-frequency
joint sparsity constraints, the proposed method has high spatial resolution and strong anti-interference ability. The proposed method
