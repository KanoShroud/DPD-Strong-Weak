QNOMP: A Quasi-Newton-Based Joint Multipath
Optimization Method for Super-Resolution
Channel Estimation and Extrapolation

 ABSTRACT Channel estimation and extrapolation are key challenges in massive multiple-input multiple-
  output communications. In this paper, we propose a quasi-Newton-based joint multipath optimization
  approach called Quasi-Newton Orthogonal Matching Pursuit (QNOMP) to address these problems. The
  algorithm comprises two stages for super-resolution recovery: first, we perform a computationally efficient
  on-grid OMP estimation of channel parameters in the sparsity domain (e.g., delay or angle); then, an off-grid
  optimization is carried out to achieve super-resolution. In the off-grid stage, we employ the quasi-Newton
 method to jointly estimate all multipath parameters, rather than optimizing them one at a time as in the
  Newtonized OMP approach. This joint estimation improves both computational efficiency and accuracy.
  Moreover, we derive an optimal extrapolation solution based on the linear minimum mean squared error
  criterion, establish its connection to the Slepian basis, and present a practical algorithm for extrapolation
  using the QNOMP results. We also propose a specialized approach that exploits the inherent block sparsity
  of the considered channels. Numerical experiments on simulated models and clustered delay line channels
  demonstrate the superior performance and low computational complexity of QNOMP.


 INDEX TERMS Quasi-Newton OMP, optimal extrapolation, slepian basis, block sparsity, super-resolution.
