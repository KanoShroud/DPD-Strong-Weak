In sparse reconstruction, the dictionary matrix should be known and accurate; otherwise, the reconstruction accuracy will be reduced due to basis mismatch.

[PDF p.12] Building upon the TDOA-based DPD framework, this paper proposes a DPD method with parametric dictionary learning for off-grid radiation sources. The presented approach simultaneously addresses both off-grid errors and channel attenuation errors by formulating the overcomplete dictionary matrix as a function of both grid points and attenuation parameters. Within the SBL framework, joint optimization of parametric dictionary learning and sparse signal reconstruction is achieved. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves over 20% improvement in localization accuracy compared to existing methods under low SNR.
