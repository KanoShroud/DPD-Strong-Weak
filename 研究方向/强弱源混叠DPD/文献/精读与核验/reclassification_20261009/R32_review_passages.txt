[PDF p.1] It is well known that the direct position determination (D- PD) method outperforms the most common two-step local- ization method when the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) is low. The advantage comes from the fact that the DPD method avoids estimating the intermediate parameters for localiza- tion. However, the DPD method has heavy computation load because of depending on exhaustive searching, especially in the multiple sources localization scenario where the problem is a high-dimensional optimization problem. In this paper, we constructed a cost function using the orthogonal relationship between received signals and noise. We reveal that the na- ture of the cost function is to verify the column-correlation of the matrix. Finally, we got the Determinant-based cost func- tion which is more efﬁcient and requires less computation re- sources in searching phase.
