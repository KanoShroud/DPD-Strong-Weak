Abstract
Mutual coupling (MC) between array elements can lead to severe accuracy degradation in
direction‐of‐arrival (DOA) estimation. In this study, an off‐grid compressed sensing (CS)
approach for DOA estimation in the presence of unknown MC is proposed using block
Newtonised orthogonal matching pursuit (NOMP). First, the model of DOA estimation
with MC is block sparsely represented to fully utilise the whole array aperture. Then, a
block NOMP algorithm with multiple measurement vectors (MMV) is developed to
achieve the estimates of DOA, in which the Newton refinement procedure is presented
by deriving the first‐ and second‐order derivatives of the block steering matrix with
respect to the angle grid. Compared with the subspace‐based and sparse recovery‐based
methods with MC, the proposed block NOMP approach has robustness in a small
number of snapshots and owns no grid‐mismatch effect. Meanwhile, in comparison to
several existing off‐grid DOA estimation methods with MC, it can achieve higher esti-
mation accuracy and similarly low computational complexity. The superior performance
of the proposed approach is shown via numerical simulations.

INTRODUCTION
As a key technology in array signal processing, direction‐of‐
arrival (DOA) estimation has received extensive attention in ra-
dar, sonar, communication, and radio astronomy etc [1]. Many
classical DOA estimation methods have been put forward over
the past few decades such as Capon, MUSIC and ESPRIT etc
[2]. In DOA estimation, we usually assume the ideal situation
that the array manifold is exactly known and has a determined
expression. However, such assumption is invalid in practice due
to array mutual coupling (MC), which is usually caused by in-
teractions among sensor array elements. Unknown MC may
cause severe distortion and degradation in estimation accuracy
[3]. To eliminate the MC effect, many array calibration tech-
niques have been proposed. Most of them [4–15] are based on
subspace techniques (e.g., MUSIC, ESPRIT). For example, in
[11], by adding auxiliary array elements, DOA estimation with
unknown MC was formulated as a normal DOA estimation
problem, and the ESPRIT algorithm was then applied directly. In
[12], an effective DOA estimation and tracking method with MC
was presented by parameterising the steering vector and using
the MUSIC‐like subspace principle. Nevertheless, the perfor-
mance of the subspace‐based algorithms deteriorate under a
small number of measurements (snapshots) and coherent
sources.
Sparse recovery technology provides an alternative strategy
for DOA estimation with unknown MC. Recently, several
sparse recovery‐based methods have been proposed [16–21].
For instance, in [17], by discarding some array elements, a
sparse representation method based on l1 norm minimisation
has been proposed for DOA estimation with MC. In [18], by
block sparsity representation, a block l1 norm method has been
presented for DOA estimation with MC. However, in the
above‐mentioned sparse methods, the spatial domain needs to
be divided into grids, thus the grid‐mismatch problem cannot
be overcome. To solve such a problem, several off‐grid sparse
recovery methods [22, 23] with MC have been proposed. For
example, in [23], a sparse Bayesian learning method with first‐

Orthogonal matching pursuit (OMP) [24] is an efficiently
iterative greedy algorithm, which chooses the column of
observation matrix that is most correlated to the current re-
sidual at each step. Unfortunately, as an on‐grid compressed
sensing (CS) method, the OMP suffers from the grid‐mismatch
effect. Newtonised orthogonal matching pursuit (NOMP),
proposed in [25] for frequency estimation, can overcome the
disadvantage of the OMP method by the Newton refinement
procedure and enjoys low computational complexity. Most
importantly, NOMP does not need the adjustment of regular-
isation parameters.
Motivated by [25], in this study, we address the off‐grid
CS problem for DOA estimation in the presence of un-
known MC, and propose a block NOMP algorithm. First, we
represent the signal model of DOA estimation as a block
sparse form in order to fully utilise the whole array aperture
in the presence of MC, where each potential DOA corre-
sponds to a block matrix. Then, we extend the original
NOMP to its block sparse version with multiple measurement
vectors (MMV) for DOA estimation. In the proposed block
NOMP algorithm, we estimate the initial DOA by finding the
most similar DOA value in the predefined block grid of DOA
based on the maximum likelihood algorithm. Then, we
gradually refine the estimates of DOAs by a block Newton
method.
The main contribution of our study is as follows: (a) we
propose a block NOMP approach for off‐grid DOA estimation
in the presence of unknown mutual coupling. The proposed
approach can overcome the grid‐mismatch effect of the exist-
ing sparse recovery methods and has relatively low computa-
tional complexity; (b) unlike the original NOMP algorithm in
[25], the proposed block NOMP algorithm is applicable to the
MMV case, whose single‐source observation model contains a
block steering matrix rather than a steering vector, thus the
matrix product rather than vector product is utilised. In addi-
tion, the first‐ and second‐order derivatives of the block
steering matrix with respect to angle grid are derived in the
block Newton refinement procedure.
The remainder of this studypaper is organised as follows. In
section 2, the signal model of DOA estimation with MC is
presented. In Section 3, the block sparsity representation of the
observed signals are formulated. In section 4, a block NOMP
algorithm for off‐grid DOA estimation in the presence of
unknown MC is proposed for block sparse recovery. Several
numerical simulation results are provided to verify the pro-
posed method in section 5.

BLOCK NOMP FOR OFF‐GRID DOA
ESTIMATION IN THE PRESENCE OF
MUTUAL COUPLING
Since the original NOMP algorithm cannot be directly
applied to the block sparsity representation model in Equa-
tion (9), in this section, we address to extending NOMP to
its block sparsity version, that is, block NOMP to estimate
the DOAs in the presence of unknown MC. Until now,
NOMP, as a fast and easy‐implemented off‐grid CS method
to overcome the grid‐mismatch effect, has not been explored
for solving the block sparsity recovery problem. Based on
Equation (9) and the original NOMP in [25], we explore a
block NOMP algorithm with MMV for block sparsity re-
covery and estimation of θ, mainly with the following three
steps in each iteration:
1. Initial estimation of a single‐source DOA based on the over‐
complete dictionary.
2. Local block Newton refinement based on the estimated
DOA in step 1.
3. Global cyclic feedback refinement based on Newton
method for all the DOAs of previously estimated sources.
In the following subsection, we provide a single‐source
DOA estimation procedure using the proposed block NOMP
algorithm as an example.

CONCLUSION
In this study, an off‐grid DOA estimation approach in the
presence of unknown mutual coupling is proposed based on
block Newtonised orthogonal matching pursuit. It can fully
utilise the whole array aperture to eliminate the mutual
coupling effect. Compared with the subspace‐based and
sparse recovery‐based methods with mutual coupling, the
proposed block NOMP approach has robustness in a small
number of snapshots and owns no grid‐mismatch effect.
Meanwhile, compared with several existing off‐grid methods
with mutual coupling, it can achieve higher estimation accu-
racy and similarly low computational complexity. Therefore, it
has potential under large array and large number of mea-
surements
for
DOA
estimation
with
unknown
mutual
coupling.
