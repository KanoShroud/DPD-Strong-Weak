[PDF p.3] The computational load is dominated by the coarse search and iterative refinement. For general sparse arrays, the coarse search scales as O(KGcN), while analytic refinement requires O(IKN) for K sources and I average iterations. RELAX decoupling also introduces a linear overhead O(N). Overall, this complexity is substantially lower than cubic subspace methods (O(N 3)) and avoids the dense dictionaries (G ≫Gc) needed for standard grid-based recovery. By achieving off-grid accuracy using a relatively coarse initial grid, the method supports low-latency processing suitable for automotive sparse apertures [5].
