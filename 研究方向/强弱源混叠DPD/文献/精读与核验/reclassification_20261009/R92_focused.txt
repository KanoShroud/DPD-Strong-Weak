[PDF p.7] The proposed Algorithm 1 in its present form is not amenable to the parametric problem in (33). Mathematically, the approach can be readily developed by modifying equation (7) to reﬂect optimization over the continuous parameter space rather than the index set. However, this step when implemented will re- quire a grid search though the grid is not predetermined. As a practical implementation of this concept, we adopt a two- step approach where in step-1 we recover an on-grid support set using Algorithm 1. This is followed by step-2 where we locally optimize the recovered grid points over a smaller pa- rameter space. This step is inspired by a previous work by the authors [56] on batch EM-SBL where all components of γ are simultaneously updated. In contrast to [56], this paper proposes a ﬁxed iterations strategy to identify a sparse solution, with an emphasis on computational complexity reduction. Such a two step approach offers ﬂexibility, as the second step can be specialized to any parametric measurement model, while the ﬁrst step remains generally applicable. This effort also offers a direct counterpart to the existing work that extends OMP to support gridless parameter estimation [57].
