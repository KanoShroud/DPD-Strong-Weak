[PDF p.3] In order to verify the robustness of the network after training to the small-scale fading of channel, the βlp in Equation (1) is selected to fol- low the Rice fading model [19] when generating testing samples. In Rice model, K is the energy factor and represents the ratio of transmission energy between the line-of-sight (LOS) path and the other non-line-of- sight (NLOS) paths in wireless channel. When K approaches 0, it is a Rayleigh fading channel. While K approaches positive inﬁnity, the chan- nel is a Gaussian channel. The simulation results are shown in Figure 4. It can be seen that CCNN exhibits strong robustness to the small-scale fading.
